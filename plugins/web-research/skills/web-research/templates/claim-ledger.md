# Claim ledger

Optional for substantial research or a refresh. Extend the existing claim map
instead of keeping duplicate records. Stable IDs are useful when claims recur.
Retain references to consulted passages, not unnecessary sensitive source content.

## Scope

- Question/decision and prior brief version, if refreshing:
- Applicable date, geography, software version, population/workload, and units:
- Claims selected for this check and reason:
- Claims outside this check; do not describe them as currently verified:

## Checked claims

| Claim ID | Statement and fact/assertion/inference type | Consulted source, passage, and direct link | Source dates, retrieval date, applicability, and limits | Current support or refresh outcome | Decision effect and unresolved evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

For refresh outcomes use confirmed current, changed, withdrawn, or unresolved.
For changed claims retain the relevant prior statement and replacement. Explain
withdrawal evidence; an inaccessible source alone means unresolved. Keep unchecked
claims labeled not checked, separate from these outcomes. New research may use
supported or unresolved claim status without pretending an earlier check exists.

For a quantitative claim, include denominator, unit, population, period, baseline,
and uncertainty. Mark derived values and supply input citations plus arithmetic.

## Coverage and conclusion changes

- Checked claims and supporting evidence:
- Unchecked areas and access failures:
- Changed calculations, comparisons, or recommendations:
- Remaining uncertainty and next evidence needed:

Deliver in the conversation unless saving is authorized. A ledger does not start
recurring monitoring or authorize external publication.
