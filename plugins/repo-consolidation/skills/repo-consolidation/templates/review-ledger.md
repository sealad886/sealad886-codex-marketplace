# PR review ledger

Copy into the task's established evidence location. Keep secrets/private diffs
out of public PRs. Use links or secured local evidence rather than transcripts.

## Identity and authority

- Repository/common Git directory and provider:
- Human request, mode, grants/restrictions, exclusions:
- Verified remote/push destination; PR URL/head repository/ref:
- Destination/base SHA; current head SHA:
- Round budget (default ten); current round; active phase:
- Required checks/approvals/queue; tool/access limits:

## Source state

| Source | Configured/required | Trigger/automatic events | Active/retired | Request/time/head/base | Explicit outcome and result ID | Coverage limits |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Independent review or configured provider | Evidence of requirement | Accepted request is not completion | Retired clean or unavailable separately | Exact identities | Pending/actionable/clean/Copilot explicit unavailable/blocker | Exact reviewed SHA; earlier scope retained |

For Copilot unavailability record stated reason, attributable cannot-run evidence,
request/preflight, time, attempted SHA, no-review-coverage status, and whether an
explicit requirement blocks integration. Do not erase earlier/late findings.

## Finding and pattern dispositions

| Finding/thread IDs | Source/round/revision | Invariant/root cause | Local/regional/global evidence and classified matches | Scope/repair boundary | Action/reply/follow-up | Proof/status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Stable IDs | Exact attribution | Falsifiable claim | Source-confirmed matches | In-scope or preservation/direction | Every thread accounted for | Check/revision/outcome |

## Final independent check and integration

- Final head/base, reviewer context, report, explicit outcome, remaining findings:
- Retired-source historical coverage; no automatic reactivation:
- CI/required approvals/conflict/queue evidence:
- Expected-head merge request; actual merged-state/destination readback:

## Cleanup and resume

| Candidate | Authority/current tip/owner | Integration and valuable-file evidence | Action/time | Readback or preservation reason |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Exact branch/worktree | Fresh identity | No unaccounted work | Eligible action only | Actual result |

Record completed/total rounds and units, last head/base, pending requests/checks,
sequencer/conflict state, recovery material, decisions, and next safe action.
Resume by inspecting live state; do not blindly repeat merges or deletions.
