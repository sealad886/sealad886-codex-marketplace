# Translation project brief

For Index-family work, record the selected profile/route; approved mandatory
requirements, preferences, translation treatment, genre/domain and contextual
background; term-concept scope; and actual owner clarifications with attribution.
Keep proposals and raw reference passages separate. Follow the
[common model-input guide](../references/index-inputs.md) and selected profile
recipe; these records are not extra MCP or engine-configuration fields.

- Project identifier and owner:
- Purpose, audience, domain, consequence of error:
- Source files, source locale, approved revision/digest and completeness inventory:
- Target locales/scripts and required product variants:
- Approved source format and extraction/verification evidence:
- Deliverable formats and final presentation/product/media checks:
- Adaptation rights, preserved facts, names, numbers, units and functional tokens:
- Reference hierarchy; glossary/style/UI resource identities and revisions:
- Local engine endpoint/model ID, connection check and representative pair results:
- Optional generation settings and requested per-translation output limits:
- Approved host-agent/inference data flow and permitted disclosures:
- Required self-check, independent AI/human revision and specialist coverage:
- Actual client content owner and release/acceptance authority:
- Schedule, query deadlines, capacity and two-round escalation rule:
- Caller-managed result/evidence retention, working assets and correction procedure:
- Current job; superseded predecessor jobs and reasons:

Unresolved material decisions stay visible. The PM does not fill a human approval
field by guessing or assigning an agent the human's name.
