Connect iCloud Mail

Your password stays in this Mac’s Keychain. Your Mac connects directly to Apple to check and use your mailbox.

Your mail stays on iCloud. This plugin does not keep a local mail archive, cache, or search index. Mail you request through Codex is returned to Codex for processing in your conversation.

Use an Apple app-specific password.
This is not your normal Apple Account password that you use to sign in to Apple.
  1. Open your Apple Account and sign in to the account that owns your iCloud mailbox.
  2. Choose Sign-In and Security → App-Specific Passwords → Generate an app-specific password. Two-factor authentication must be enabled.
  3. Name it “Codex iCloud Mail”, then paste the generated password below.

Use your primary iCloud Mail address (including an existing @me.com or @mac.com address). Your Apple Account sign-in email may be different.

Paste the 16-letter password generated by Apple. Do not enter your Apple Account password.

This connection page expires after 10 minutes. macOS may ask you to allow Keychain access. Connecting checks incoming and outgoing mail access without sending a message.
