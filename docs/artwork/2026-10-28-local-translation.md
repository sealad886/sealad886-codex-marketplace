## Square logo

Final file: `plugins/local-translation/assets/logo.png`

1254 × 1254 PNG, below 5 MiB. The generated result was inspected for legible A and 文 glyphs, brand consistency and clear silhouette.

```text
Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: original square marketplace plugin logo for Local Translation, a professional translation workflow with expert-agent collaboration.
Primary request: create a polished, distinctive, minimal brand symbol: two interlocking conversation-page shapes, one ivory and one warm amber, their overlapping forms suggesting an open book and a clear exchange between specialists. The ivory conversation page carries one crisp dark-teal Latin capital A; the amber conversation page carries one crisp dark-teal Chinese character 文. Give both marks generous optical weight and precise geometry. Flat vector-like graphic with subtle paper depth, no photorealism.
Scene/backdrop: solid deep teal #133D4B square field with softly rounded corners. Keep the corners clean; the logo itself fills the central 65 percent with generous margin.
Composition: centered, balanced, square 1:1, strong silhouette recognizable at 48 pixels, cream shape upper-left and amber shape lower-right, both communicating as one unified mark. Refined editorial software identity.
Lighting/mood: flat graphic lighting with extremely restrained dimensional shading, calm and professional.
Palette: deep teal #133D4B, pale ivory #F4F1E8, warm amber #F5BE68. No rainbow.
Text: only A and 文, no company name, no other lettering.
Constraints: original artwork, no borrowed brand logos, no robots, no padlocks, no checkmarks, no flags, no maps, no certification marks, no watermarks, no thin detail, no mockup or framing. Deliver one finished square logo image.
```

```text
Primary request: create a sophisticated original editorial software banner for professional translation and collaboration among expert agents. Scene/backdrop: uninterrupted deep teal #133D4B field, subtly illuminated and richly calm. Faint elegant connecting curves between a handful of small ivory/amber editorial page forms on the right suggest specialists exchanging and reviewing content. Visual network is restrained and purposeful.
Composition: broad horizontal layout around 3:1, generous margins. Left half contains only the brand title, stacked in two lines in large refined contemporary sans-serif lettering: Local on first line, Translation on second. Right half contains the branded interlocking conversation pages A and 文, with smaller document/page forms at different depths behind it. Keep title highly legible at README width. Overall professional, understated, high design quality.
Lighting: soft graphic studio illumination, slight paper edge shadows, no lens flare.
Color palette: deep teal #133D4B background, pale ivory #F4F1E8 title and paper, warm amber #F5BE68 accents. Clean typography.

Constraints: no subtitle, no invented slogan, no robots, no padlock, no flags, no globe, no corporate provider logos, no checkmark or certification mark, no people, no UI screenshot, no watermark, no photographic mockup. Finished banner artwork, no border.
```
